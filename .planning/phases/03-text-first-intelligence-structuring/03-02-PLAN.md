# Phase 3 Plan 02: Structured Knowledge Extraction Engine & Resource Mention Parser

## Objective

- **What:** Implement the core `StructuringService` and heuristic/LLM resource mention extractor to convert cleaned transcripts and video metadata into structured takeaways and identified technical resources.
- **Why:** Raw transcripts are verbose and hard to scan. Users need structured takeaways, categorized tags, and explicit lists of software tools/repos mentioned in the short.
- **Output:** `structuringService.ts`, `resourceExtractor.ts`, service exports in `index.ts`, and unit tests.
