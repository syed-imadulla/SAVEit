# Phase 2 Plan 01: Retrieval Feasibility Benchmark & Empirical Report

## Objective

- **What:** Create a reproducible retrieval test suite and benchmark script to empirically test public Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts across 4 categories (speech-heavy, code/text-heavy, captioned, inaccessible), documenting real-world accessibility in `BENCHMARK_REPORT.md`.
- **Why:** Before writing adapter code, we must empirically establish what metadata, captions, and media are legitimately accessible without tripping bot protection or violating platform terms.
- **Output:** Test corpus fixture, executable benchmark script, and published `BENCHMARK_REPORT.md`.
